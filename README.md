A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-golden-heron-44-fortune-in-a-pot-ded981e9) entry for **Fortune in a Pot**: one kind, funny fortune for the houseplant on the windowsill, in a single line of at most 120 characters.

## The fortune

> By Sunday, the sun will return to your windowsill, and you'll grow one new leaf just to make your keeper smile.

111 characters — a prediction with the cadence of a fortune, written for this plant in this window: the sun coming back to its windowsill, and the plant answering with one new leaf, purely to make its keeper smile.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes the fortune to `/output/entry.txt`, the file the gardener judge reads.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
